OCI card in lieu of lost or damaged card: physical OCI cards discontinued

Lost or damaged OCI card and the e-OCI

With effect from 8 July 2026, printing of physical OCI cards/booklets has been discontinued. Accordingly, no physical OCI card will be dispatched for OCI applications processed under the new system.

OCI cardholders may download their e-OCI by logging into their account on the OCI portal (https://ociservices.gov.in/onlineOCI/). The downloaded e-OCI is the valid OCI document and may be used for travel to India along with the valid foreign passport linked to the OCI.

Information provided by the Consulate General of India, Atlanta, for the DOST assistant. Official OCI services: ociservices.gov.in.
